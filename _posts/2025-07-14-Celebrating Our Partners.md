> *"I hope you are well. I want to thank you and your team for your ingenuity and dedication in creating the SmartView app for Center for Change. As a Community Health Worker (CHW/Health Coach), I have found its ease of use to be a true godsend. For the past 11 months, I’ve been working with the app designed specifically for the BP device we provide to clients. While functional, I found setup and explaining how to share readings to be the most challenging parts. This often led to lower participation rates and long delays in receiving reports—whether due to the app’s processing time or clients’ hesitation to navigate the multiple steps required to share their data. With the introduction of the SmartView app, the simplicity, clarity, and real-time visibility for both the client and the health coach are tremendous assets. This not only improves how clients approach their blood pressure management but also enhances the ability of Health Coaches to provide timely, informed support. If I could suggest any enhancements, they would be to color-code the readings and include alerts for any arrhythmias detected during measurements.
> Kudos to you and your team for such outstanding work! The future is definitely brighter with SmartView in the world."*  
— **Shari Williams**, Florida Center for Change
